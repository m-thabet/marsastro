Mars Mission Commander | Team plus c
Challenge: Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer

Space survival is hard to teach. Mars Mission Commander is a playable educational prototype designed to help young explorers practise decisions about oxygen, power, water and food. Players explore an illustrated Mars outpost, balance limited resources, and see how their choices change the mission. The demo shows the current local prototype, not a future feature or a certified mission simulator.

NASA's MOXIE demonstration informs the oxygen-production lesson; NASA OSDR RadLab's archived ISS DosTel1 observations support discussion of radiation; OSD-314 plant research informs the explanation of light and space seedlings. The four radiation measurements shown are from the ISS on 1 April 2022, not Mars. Resource rates, exposure points, growth times and events in the game are fictional teaching rules. No NASA endorsement is implied.
