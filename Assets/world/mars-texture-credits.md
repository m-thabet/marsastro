Mars surface texture: **Solar System Scope / INOVE**.

- Source: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/

- License: **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International** — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Original retained at `Assets/source/8k_mars.jpg` (8192×4096).
- Changes: resized to 4096×2048 as `mars-global-color-4k.jpg`; its color contrast was used to bake `mars-illustrative-normal-2k.png` (2048×1024).
- These are visualization derivatives. The normal map is illustrative and does not represent measured Mars elevations. The provider's source map is processed and may include reconstructed terrain.

Credit and license metadata are also embedded on the planet node in both delivered GLBs. Retain this attribution when sharing the texture derivatives or models containing them.

The nebula backdrop, star meshes, surrounding rocks, lighting setup, and scene construction are original procedural work for this project based on the user-provided visual reference. The surrounding objects and nebula are artistic presentation elements, not measured astronomical data.
